Questions

Common questions

I'm not sure what I need

Almost nobody is, at the start.

And you do not need to be. The first conversation is for exactly that: putting the situation in order, separating the urgent from the important and deciding where to start.

What does working with you cost?

You always know before we start.

The financial diagnostic starts at €550 + VAT. Everything else is quoted per engagement, at a fixed amount agreed in writing according to scope: never by the hour and never with surprises at the end. On a sale or acquisition mandate, part of the fee can be tied to closing, so that my incentive and yours point the same way.

You work alone. Do you have the capacity?

That is precisely what you are hiring.

The person who analyses, writes and sits down to negotiate is the same one, and I only take on mandates I can serve properly. When a tax, legal or technical specialist is needed I bring them in and coordinate them myself. You always have a single point of contact.

I already have an accountant and a bank

And you will go on needing them.

Your accountant records what has already happened and your bank works from its own product catalogue. Both do their job well. What I add is something else: looking at your company the way the person buying it would, but sitting on your side.

I need this to stay confidential

It is the basis of everything I do.

We sign a confidentiality agreement before I see a single figure. I work alone, I publish no client names and I discuss no transactions. Your information does not leave this conversation.
