01

Prepare

Put the company in order and build judgement before deciding anything

Financial diagnosticFrom €550 + VAT

Your situation

You have a business that works, but the big decisions —selling, bringing in a partner, investing, planning succession— are approaching and the information is scattered. There is no clear reference for what the company is worth.

What it means

Negotiating without knowing your own floor. Accepting a figure because it sounds good. Discovering in the buyer’s due diligence the problems you could have fixed with two years’ notice.

How I help

I put the numbers in order, analyse the business the way the person buying it would, and give you a reasoned read: what it is worth, what sustains that value, what the risks are and what can be improved before you move.

Services

  • Financial diagnostic
  • Company valuation
  • Independent opinion
  • Internal due diligence
  • Value creation plan

Outcome

A value range you can defend, the risks named one by one and a short list of decisions. With no obligation to act on any of it.

02

Execute

A major transaction, with a single financial adviser running it

Your situation

There is a transaction on the table: an offer received, an interested partner, a financing need or a structure that no longer fits the size of the business.

What it means

A long process, with legal and tax advisers, deadlines, documentation and a counterparty who probably does this several times a year. Every point left unattended is paid for in price or in warranties.

How I help

I run the process from start to finish: I prepare the documentation, filter counterparties, build the value argument, negotiate sitting on your side and coordinate the other advisers through to signing.

Services

  • Sale and acquisition of companies
  • Bringing in a partner
  • Financing
  • Restructuring

Outcome

A transaction closed on terms you understand and can explain, with one person accountable for getting it there.

03

Support

Permanent financial judgement, without putting a finance director on the payroll

Your situation

The company has grown enough to need financial judgement, but not enough to justify a full-time finance director. You make the big calls alone, with the accountant on one side and the bank on the other.

What it means

Budgets built too late, financing negotiated from weakness, investments decided on instinct, and a company that is never quite ready for the transaction that will eventually come.

How I help

I step in as your outsourced finance director, on an agreed level of commitment. It is not one report a year: it is having someone who knows your numbers and with whom you can think out loud before deciding.

Services

  • Outsourced finance director
  • Regular meetings and reporting
  • Budget, forecast and control
  • Banking and financing
  • Support on investment and acquisition decisions
  • Preparing for a future sale

Outcome

An owner who decides with the numbers in front of him, and a company that will not have to improvise when the moment comes.

Working method

The mechanical work —consolidating, reconciling, cross-checking, reviewing— is automated. That means more information reviewed, in more depth and in less time, and patterns a manual review would miss. The time saved goes into what cannot be automated: thinking through your case and talking it over with you.

Questions

What owners usually ask me

And if a doubt remains, we clear it up in a conversation

I'm not sure what I need

Almost nobody is, at the start.

And you do not need to be. The first conversation is for exactly that: putting the situation in order, separating the urgent from the important and deciding whether there is work to do. If I think there is not, I will say so.

What does working with you cost?

You always know before we start.

The financial diagnostic starts at €550 + VAT. Everything else is quoted per engagement, at a fixed amount agreed in writing according to scope: never by the hour and never with an invoice that surprises you at the end. On a sale or acquisition mandate, part of the fee can be tied to closing, so that my incentive and yours point the same way.

You work alone. Do you have the capacity?

That is precisely what you are hiring.

At a large firm your deal is sold by a partner and executed by an analyst two years out of university. Here the person who analyses, writes and sits down to negotiate is the same one, and I only take on mandates I can serve properly. When a tax, legal or technical specialist is needed I bring them in and coordinate them myself; you still have a single point of contact.

I already have an accountant and a bank

And you will go on needing them.

Your accountant records what has already happened and your bank makes its living selling you product; both do their job well, but neither looks at your company the way the person buying it would. That perspective, the other side’s but sitting on yours, is all I add, and it competes with nobody you already have.

I need this to stay confidential

It is the basis of everything I do.

We sign a confidentiality agreement before I see a single figure. I work alone, I publish no client names and I discuss no transactions. Your information does not leave this conversation.
