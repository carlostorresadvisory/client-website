Quality of earnings

The two findings pointed in opposite directions

QoE and valuation of a family-owned engineering firm. In the P&L, a fleet purchase expensed as repairs and debt-arrangement costs booked as operating expense were depressing EBITDA. And the execution-consulting mix makes any single year unrepresentative.

The balance sheet told the opposite story: €1.4M of receivables owed by no customer, shareholder withdrawals never settled, an apparent DSO of 394 days. Cleaned up: 72, with the tax contingency identified and settled voluntarily before any inquiry.
